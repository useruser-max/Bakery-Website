Our Story

Welcome to The Hidden Cup Bakery – your cozy corner for homemade happiness. Founded in 2010 in the heart of Karachi, our bakery started as a dream to bring people together through the warmth of freshly baked treats.

From buttery croissants to decadent cakes and colorful tarts, every item is made with love, tradition, and a little touch of magic. Our team works every day to ensure that your experience is as delightful as the aroma that greets you when you walk in.

We believe that good food brings good memories – and we’re here to help you make some sweet ones!
