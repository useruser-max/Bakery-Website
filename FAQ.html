What are your store hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. On Saturdays, we open from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays.

Do you offer vegan options?

Yes, we offer a variety of vegan cakes, cookies, and pastries. Please check our menu for more details!

Can I place a custom cake order?

Absolutely! You can place custom cake orders by contacting us through our booking page or calling us directly.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes, we offer delivery for orders within a 10-mile radius. Delivery charges may apply depending on location.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes, we offer gluten-free cakes and cookies. Please let us know about your dietary needs when placing an order.
